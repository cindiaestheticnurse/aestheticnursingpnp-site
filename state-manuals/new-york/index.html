New York Med Spa Policy and Procedure Manual

State-Specific Policies for Aesthetic Nurses, NPs, PAs and Practice Owners · Cindi Vokey, BSN, RN, LNC

Sources Reviewed September 2026

In New York, RNs carry out patient-specific medical regimens ordered by authorized practitioners, and the State Board for Medicine has published guidance on energy devices and lasers. Your New York manual is organized around these rules.

Who Regulates Aesthetic Practice in New York

  • New York State Board for Nursing
  • New York State Board for Medicine
  • NYS Department of Health, Regulated Medical Waste
  • Federal OSHA (Bloodborne Pathogens)

Key New York Rules Your Manual Addresses

Patient-Specific Orders

New York's nursing practice FAQ states that an RN may carry out a medical regimen prescribed for a specific patient by a physician, NP, PA or other authorized practitioner, but may not diagnose or prescribe (NYS Nursing Practice Information FAQ).

Nurse Practitioner Practice

The same FAQ states that NPs practice under a written practice agreement and protocols with a collaborating physician unless they have more than 3,600 qualifying practice hours (NYS Nursing Practice Information FAQ).

Energy Devices and Lasers

The State Board for Medicine's 2025 guidance states that energy-device treatment must be performed by, or on the order of, a physician, PA or NP. An RN may carry out the order under general supervision, which does not require the supervisor to be physically present (NYS Board for Medicine, Use of Energy Devices Including Lasers).

Procedures the Official Sources Don't Address Directly

Kybella, PRP, sclerotherapy and PDO threads are not specifically addressed in the official New York sources reviewed for this page. Your manual flags them for confirmation before you offer them.
