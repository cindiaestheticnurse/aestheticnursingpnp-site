State-Specific Med Spa Policy and Procedure Manuals

Generic "All 50 States" Templates Miss the Rules That Matter in Your State

Sources Reviewed September 2026

Every state regulates aesthetic nursing differently. States differ on who may perform the initial examination, how delegation and supervision must be documented, and who may operate lasers or give IV hydration. Each edition of our manual includes a state-specific regulatory section built from that state's Board and statutory sources.

Choose Your State

  • California — California aesthetic practices run on standardized procedures, physician supervision and a clear line between the physician's examination and what can be delegated to a registered nurse.
  • Texas — Texas aesthetic practice depends on physician delegation and written delegation orders.
  • Florida — Florida's rules for aesthetic nursing are spread across the Nurse Practice Act, physician office-supervision statutes and recent laws on laser hair removal and IV vitamin therapy.
  • Arizona — The Arizona State Board of Nursing has published detailed advisory opinions on medical aesthetic procedures and IV hydration.
  • New York — In New York, RNs carry out patient-specific medical regimens ordered by authorized practitioners, and the State Board for Medicine has published guidance on energy devices and lasers.

Practicing in another state? The Binder Blueprint covers every state, and you can book a free consult to discuss a custom manual.
