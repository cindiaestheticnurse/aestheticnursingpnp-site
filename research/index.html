Scope & Methodology

What This Review Does and Doesn’t Show.

  • Source: 32 Publicly Filed Licensing Board Accusations Related to Aesthetics, Reviewed in 2026.
  • Only 7 of the 32 Involved Documented Patient Harm.
  • A Case Is Counted If the Issue Appears Anywhere in Its Description or Citations; One Case Can Appear in Several Findings.
  • Cost Recovery Reimburses the Board’s Investigation and Prosecution. It Excludes Attorney Fees, Probation Monitoring, Coursework and Lost Income.
