Questions

Manual Builder FAQs.

Still Unsure? Ask Cindi or Book a Free 30-Minute Consult.

Where Does My Manual Go After I Pay?

Into My Manuals on the Aesthetic Nursing CE Site (AestheticNursingCEs.com). Use the Email for Your CE Account, or a New Email and an Account Is Set Up for You. A Download Link Is Also Emailed to You.

Can I Edit the Manual?

The Word Manual Is Fillable: Signature, Date and Updates Fields Are Editable, and the Policy Text Is Locked. Practice-Specific Changes Go in Each Policy’s Updates Section, Reviewed With Your Medical Director.

Who Needs to Sign It?

Your Medical Director and Primary Provider. Either May Sign First. Sign by Hand, With Your Own E-Signature Tool, or Add DocuSign Signing ($250) at Checkout.

What Should I Have Ready?

Your Practice Name and Address, Your Medical Director’s and Primary Provider’s Names and Credentials Exactly as They Should Print, Your Treatment Menu and Your Logo (PNG or JPG, Up to 5 MB).

Does the Manual Guarantee Compliance?

No. It Is Built From State-Specific Content and Your Practice Details to Support Your Compliance Work. It Is Not Approved or Endorsed by Any Licensing Board, Agency or Accrediting Body. Review It Against Current State Law and Your Medical Director’s Protocols Before Use.

What If I Have Several Locations or States?

Each Manual Is One State Edition. For Several Locations or States, a Custom Manual or Growth-Ready Support May Fit Better. Book a Free Consult.
