How This Is Estimated

Attorney time is priced at published healthcare-attorney rates ($350–$500 an hour). Your time uses national wage and owner-income data. Risk uses general-nursing complaint and discipline rates and the published average cost of a license defense. No published data covers aesthetic nurses specifically, and the share of risk that good policies remove has never been measured, so we assume 30% and show the result small, with its probability, never alone.

Estimates for planning only, based on published averages and the answers above; not a prediction of your outcome.
