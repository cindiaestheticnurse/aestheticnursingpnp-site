Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how Aesthetic Nursing Policy & Procedures, Inc. ("Aesthetic Nursing P&P," "we," "us") collects, uses and shares information when you visit aestheticnursingpnp.com, use the Binder Blueprint, request the Accusation Review Findings Brief, contact us, or build and buy a manual through the Manual Builder on this site or at theaestheticmanual.com (together, the "Sites"). We are based in San Diego, California.

Our CE courses at aestheticnursingces.com and any consulting engagement may have their own terms or agreements. Where those differ from this policy, they control for that service.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Server logs. Our hosting and network providers (GitHub Pages, Render and Cloudflare) automatically log technical details such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and the time of the request, to deliver and protect the Sites.
  • Fonts. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser details when the fonts load.
  • Site analytics. The main site (aestheticnursingpnp.com) uses Google Analytics to measure visits: pages viewed, how you arrived (for example, from a search engine), approximate location at the city level, device and browser type, and when a form is submitted or a file is downloaded. We send only the type of action, such as "consult form submitted." We never send the names, emails, phone numbers or notes you type into our forms. We have turned off Google's advertising features for our Analytics account, and Google uses this information to provide the Analytics service to us.
  • Cookies. Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (named _ga and _ga_ followed by an ID) to recognize repeat visits. They are kept for up to 14 months and are not used for advertising. The main site sets no other cookies. The Manual Builder uses one essential cookie to keep you signed in when you use an access code or admin password. Stripe's checkout page sets its own cookies to process payment and prevent fraud.

We do not use advertising trackers or retargeting pixels on the Sites. To turn off Google Analytics on this browser, select the button below. You can also install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Please Do Not Send Patient Information

The Sites are built for practice and business information, not patient information. Please do not enter or upload protected health information (PHI) or any patient details in our forms, the Manual Builder or by email. If a consulting engagement requires access to PHI, we will put a separate written agreement in place first, including a Business Associate Agreement where HIPAA requires one.

How We Use Information

  • To answer your questions, schedule consults and provide the services you ask for.
  • To send the Findings Brief you request.
  • To build your manual, process payment, create or update your Aesthetic Nursing CEs account, add the manual to My Manuals, and send account, order and download emails.
  • To keep records of orders and transactions for accounting, tax and legal purposes.
  • To follow up about your request. If you'd like no further follow-up, tell us and we'll stop.
  • To protect the Sites, prevent fraud or misuse, and enforce our Terms of Use.
  • To comply with law and respond to lawful requests.

How Long We Keep Information

  • Unpaid Manual Builder orders (including the uploaded logo) are deleted automatically after 48 hours.
  • Download links from My Manuals expire after a few minutes. Direct backup download links, used only if the dashboard connection fails, expire after 30 days.
  • Encrypted backups are kept for about 31 days, then removed automatically.
  • Completed orders and manual files are kept so we can reissue your manual and meet accounting and tax requirements, generally for up to seven years, unless you ask us to delete them sooner and no law requires us to keep them.
  • Messages and form submissions are kept in our email for as long as needed to serve you and maintain business records.

How We Protect Information

The Sites use encrypted connections (HTTPS). The Manual Builder keeps the master template and order files on a private server, protects administrative tools with a password, and uses signed, expiring download links, and limits each My Manuals download to the signed-in account owner. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we learn of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you as the law requires.

Your Choices and Rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we have about you, correct it, or delete it. Email info@aestheticnursingpnp.com or call 508-498-6604. We will confirm your request and respond within the time the law allows, generally 45 days. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we may keep information the law requires us to keep.

California Residents

Depending on whether the California Consumer Privacy Act applies to us, you may have the right to know, delete and correct personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, as those terms are defined in that law, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising any privacy right. Under California's "Shine the Light" law, you may ask whether we share personal information with third parties for their direct marketing. We do not.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

We do not track visitors across other websites for advertising, and we do not allow third parties to do so on the Sites. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, the main site does not load Google Analytics for you, and we treat the signal as a request not to sell or share your information, which we already don't do.

Children

The Sites are intended for licensed professionals and business owners. They are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Other Websites

The Sites link to other websites, such as our CE course site, Zoom and Stripe checkout. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our services change. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date. If a change materially affects how we use information we already have, we will tell you before it takes effect.
