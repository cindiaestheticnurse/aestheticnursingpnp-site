Meet Nurse Cindi

Cindi Vokey, BSN, RN: Aesthetic Nurse Consultant Who Knows the Treatment Room and the Binder.

I'm Cindi Vokey, BSN, RN, founder and lead educator of Aesthetic Nursing Policy & Procedures, Inc. My passion for the past 18 years in aesthetic nursing goes beyond injecting. After facing my own licensing issue, I made it right and promised myself I would help others in this field do the same. Since then, I've guided providers through Board investigations and supported them during probation.

Based in San Diego, I work with providers nationwide, alongside your healthcare attorney whenever legal review is needed.

My Policies and Procedures have been reviewed by State Boards as part of real Board matters.
