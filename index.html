Why Compliance Matters Now

Standardized Procedures and Documentation Before a Board Complaint.

Most Practices Don’t Discover a Gap Until Someone Asks for the Records. By Then, the Documents Either Exist or They Don’t.

01 · Complaints

Anyone Can File One

Patients, Coworkers and Others Can File a Complaint. According to the California Board of Registered Nursing, Allegations May Include Gross Negligence or Incompetence, Unprofessional Conduct and Unlicensed Activity.

02 · Accusations

An Accusation Is Public

When the Evidence Supports Discipline, the Board Files an Accusation: a Legal Document Listing Formal Charges, and a Public Record. Investigators Gather Patient Records and Personnel Records.

03 · Documentation

Documentation Gaps Come Up Often

Investigators Ask for Written Standardized Procedures, Good Faith Exams and Competency Records. Missing Documents Are One of the Most Common Findings in Aesthetic Cases.

Does Your Manual Match What Actually Happens in Your Practice?

  • If the Board Requested Your Practice Guidelines and Documentation Today, Would You Feel Confident in What You Have?
  • Are Your Policies Specific to Your State and Practice?
  • Can You Demonstrate Appropriate Medical Oversight?
  • Are Provider Competencies, Treatment Records and Protocols Documented?
  • Can Your Team Demonstrate That Written Procedures Are Followed?
