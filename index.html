Med Spa Compliance Questions

Does My Medical Spa Need Its Own Policies and Procedures Manual?

Yes. Policies should reflect your state, services, providers and supervision structure, not a generic practice.

Can I Use a Generic Med Spa Compliance Manual?

A template is a starting point. It must be customized, approved, signed and followed. No manual guarantees a regulatory outcome.

What Should a Medical Director Have in Place?

A written agreement that fits state rules, defined availability, chart review and site visit records, and backup coverage.

Do You Help Practices Prepare for Board Investigations?

We help organize policies, competency records and documentation. Legal questions should go to a healthcare attorney.

Do You Help Implement Policies and Train Staff?

Yes. Implementation, staff training and competency documentation are core parts of our consulting.

Can You Work With a Practice That Is Not Yet Open?

Yes. Building your compliance framework before your first patient is the ideal time to start.

Where Does Your Practice Stand?

Opening, expanding or strengthening an established practice, start with an assessment of your current compliance framework.
